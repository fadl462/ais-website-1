Official AIS News · August 2026

A New Vision. A New Chapter. A Brighter Future.

Following the management transition, AIS has outlined a vision focused on academic excellence, discipline, high moral values, integrity and service to humanity.

Official StatementAugust 2026School TransformationOfficial School Statement
The new direction

The new management has welcomed parents, students, teachers, staff and stakeholders to what it describes as a new and exciting chapter for Ahmadiyya International School.

The statement sets out an ambition to build a modern, safe and highly respected school while strengthening the academic, moral and service-oriented foundations of AIS.

Five priorities at the heart of the vision

The statement identifies five values that the school intends to be recognised for: Academic Excellence, Discipline, High Moral Values, Integrity and Service to Humanity.

01Academic ExcellenceStrengthening the quality and opportunities of learning.
02DisciplineBuilding a safe, orderly and purposeful school culture.
03High Moral ValuesKeeping character and values central to education.
04IntegrityPromoting responsibility, trust and high standards.
05Service to HumanityPreparing children to contribute positively to society.

Transformation already underway

The statement says the transformation has already begun. Work is described as focusing on clearing, cleaning and beautifying the school environment, alongside progressive improvements to classrooms, boarding and washroom facilities, ICT and learning resources, security, sports and recreation, and school transportation.

Investing in people

Alongside improvements to the physical and learning environment, the new management says it is recruiting qualified and dedicated teachers and rebranding AIS to reflect the new standards, opportunities and quality of education it intends to provide.

86 students at the centre

The statement places the school's 86 current students and their parents at the heart of the transformation. Its stated goal is to provide every child with a safe, conducive and inspiring environment in which to learn, grow and succeed.

Building the next chapter together

Parents, students, teachers, staff and other stakeholders are asked for continued support, trust and cooperation as the school works towards what the statement describes as a stronger AIS and a brighter future for its children.
