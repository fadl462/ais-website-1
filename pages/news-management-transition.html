AIS Management Transition Officially Authorised

12 August 2026·Official Notification

A new management and administration structure has been formally authorised for T. I. Ahmadiyya International School, Bustan-E-Ahmad.

Official AIS News12 August 2026Management & Administration
Bustan-e-Ahmad, Accra · 12 August 2026

T. I. Ahmadiyya International School has entered a formal new management phase after the National Headquarters of the Ahmadiyya Muslim Mission, Ghana, authorised the takeover and reorganisation of the school's management and administration.

The authorisation was communicated on 12 August 2026, establishing the management structure and responsibilities for the school's day-to-day operation at Bustan-E-Ahmad.

The authorisation followed a review of reorganisation proposals by the National Executive Council (AMLA). Under the new arrangement, the Ameer authorised Nana Yiadom Boakye Kanto (Mr. Ahmad Adade Boakye) to assume full responsibility for the day-to-day management and operation of AIS.

A new management responsibility

The new arrangement gives management responsibility across the school's administration and operations. The notification identifies four broad areas that will fall under this responsibility.

  • Administration and operations of the school.
  • Supervision of staff and students.
  • Academic activities and related school operations.
  • School facilities, records and finances.

A transition for the wider AIS community

The transition also has a wider community dimension. Parents, staff, students and other stakeholders are being called upon to recognise the change and extend their full support and cooperation to the new management.

The communication also expresses appreciation for the continued dedication of the school community and points to working together for the growth and excellence of the children's education.

What the authorisation means for AIS

In practical terms, the notification establishes responsibility for the school's day-to-day management and identifies the areas under that responsibility. It marks a formal administrative milestone as T. I. Ahmadiyya International School moves into its next phase.

For the AIS community, the transition begins with a clear call for cooperation: to support the new management and work together for the continued growth and excellence of the school.
