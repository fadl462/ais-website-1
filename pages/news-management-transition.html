AIS Management Transition Officially Authorised

A new management and administration structure has been formally authorised for T. I. Ahmadiyya International School, Bustan-e-Ahmad.

Official Notification12 August 2026Management & Administration
What happened

On 12 August 2026, the National Headquarters of the Ahmadiyya Muslim Mission, Ghana, communicated official authorisation for the takeover and reorganisation of the management and administration of T. I. Ahmadiyya International School, Bustan-E-Ahmad.

The communication marks a formal management transition for AIS and sets out who will assume responsibility for the school's day-to-day operation and the areas that will fall under the new management.

A new management responsibility

According to the official notification, the authorisation followed a review of reorganisation proposals by the National Executive Council (AMLA). The Ameer authorised Nana Yiadom Boakye Kanto (Mr. Ahmad Adade Boakye) to assume full responsibility for the day-to-day management and operation of the school.

Effective12 August 2026
InstitutionT. I. Ahmadiyya International School
LocationBustan-E-Ahmad

What the new management will oversee

The notification states that the new management will oversee four broad areas:

  • Administration and operations of the school.
  • Supervision of staff and students.
  • Academic activities and related school operations.
  • School facilities, records and finances.

A call for cooperation

The communication also calls on parents, staff, students and stakeholders to recognise the transition and extend their full support and cooperation to the new management.

It closes with an appreciation for the continued dedication of the school community and a commitment to working together for the growth and excellence of the children's education.
