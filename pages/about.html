Our identity

A school experience built around the whole learner.

The school’s identity is grounded in education that develops the learner as a whole. Public information identifies T.I. Ahmadiyya International School as a school of the Ahmadiyya Muslim Mission, Ghana, with a day-and-boarding model and an international mix of learners.

School at a glance

Rooted in Ashongman.
Open to a wider community.

The school is located at Bustan-e-Ahmad in the Ga East area of Accra. Public information supplied for this project describes learners from Ghana as well as countries including Nigeria, Côte d’Ivoire, Pakistan and The Gambia.
