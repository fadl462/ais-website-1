About AIS

More than a school. A foundation for life.

AIS is presented as a school where academic excellence, character, confidence, discipline and responsibility develop together.

Our identity

Best Among Equals.

The school’s identity is grounded in education that develops the learner as a whole. Public information identifies T.I. Ahmadiyya International School as a school of the Ahmadiyya Muslim Mission, Ghana, with a day-and-boarding model and an international mix of learners.

School at a glance

Rooted in Ashongman. Open to a wider community.

The school is located at Bustan-e-Ahmad in the Ga East area of Accra. Public information supplied for this project describes the school as day and boarding, established in September 2005, with learners from Ghana as well as countries including Nigeria, Côte d’Ivoire, Pakistan and The Gambia.
