Welcome to AIS

More Than a School.
A Foundation for Life.

T.I. Ahmadiyya International School is a day and boarding basic school at Bustan-e-Ahmad, between Agbogba and Old Ashongman in Accra. Established in September 2005, the school serves learners from Ghana and a wider international community while combining core academics with character, discipline and purposeful school life.

AIS at a glance
